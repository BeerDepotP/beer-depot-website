# The Beer Depot website — how we work

- Owner: The Beer Depot, Pewaukee WI. Domain: beerdepotpewaukee.com (bought through Netlify 2026-11-03). Hosted on Netlify (project "cute-salamander-50d302"), deployed from the `main` branch of this repo.
- **Every push to `main` is a Netlify production deploy and costs 15 credits.** The owner asked that changes be batched.
- Do all work on the `drafts` branch. Push `drafts` freely (it does not publish).
- Only merge `drafts` into `main` and push when the owner explicitly says to publish.
- Preview drafts without a deploy: run `python3 tools/build.py` and show `preview.html` (sample stock).
- Edit `tools/index.template.html`, then run `python3 tools/build.py` to regenerate `public/index.html`. Do not hand-edit `public/index.html`.
- Live stock comes from KORONA via `netlify/functions/stock.mjs`, which serves a saved copy from Blobs ("stock-latest") instantly and refreshes it in the background when over 60s old; `stock-refresh.mjs` also rebuilds it every 10 min, 6 AM–10 PM Central. Section rules live at the top of that file. Check sorting with `/api/stock?report=<REPORT_KEY>`.
- Store Tools at /food-stock (PIN in FOOD_PIN): tabs Food Stock (switches, Netlify Blobs; every item has an Edit button opening a drop-down for name, prices and picture: food-stock blob `edits[key]` {label, photo} + `prices[key]`, photos in Blobs "food-photo-<id>" served by GET /api/food-stock?photo=<id>, helpers menuLabel/menuPrices/menuPhoto at the bottom of tools/menu.js; pizza toppings list is editable (Add/Remove, blob `toppings`, no pictures); Add-Ons (gyro) and pellet flavors are not editable in the drop-down; jump buttons at the top for each food group, maker and Grilling & Outdoor), Sales (On Sale section at the top of the site + red On Sale chip; on/off switch, items with photo/name/sale price/regular price/note, "Save $X" computed; sales.mjs, Blobs "sales" + "sale-photo-<id>", photos shrunk in the browser), Seasonal (after Sales; on/off switch hides it completely; one editable name used for the menu-bar chip, Browse drawer and section heading; items with photo/name/price/note; seasonal.mjs, Blobs "seasonal" + "seasonal-photo-<id>"; section sits right after On Sale), Couldn't Find It, Top Searches, Requests (each row has an × to remove it: insights.mjs type "remove"; Top Searches deletes the term for that week, Couldn't Find It only clears its no-result count, Requests deletes the request), Bourbon Club, with unread badges. No deploy needed for switch changes. Menu data: tools/menu.js. Functions: food-stock.mjs, insights.mjs, shared PIN check in netlify/lib/pin.mjs.
- Hours: Store Tools tab Hours (normal weekly hours + One-Day Changes for a date: closed or short hours + note; past dates drop automatically so it reverts the day after). hours.mjs (GET /api/hours public, Blob "hours" {weekly,overrides}); website HOURS in the template is the default until /api/hours loads (weekly null = defaults), hoursFor(offset) merges overrides, notice bar #hours-note shows overrides within 14 days. JSON-LD hours in <head> stay static: update if weekly hours change.

Blobs "notify" {wants,tokens} + "notify-state" {on,delayMin,inSince,sent}; helpers in netlify/lib/notify.mjs; sendMail() in club-email.mjs (Brevo). Store Tools tab "Back in Stock": on/off (off hides buttons and stops emails), wait time, who waits for what (with Remove/Clear), recently sent.
