# The Beer Depot website — how we work

- Owner: The Beer Depot, Pewaukee WI. Domain: beerdepotpewaukee.com (bought through Netlify 2026-11-03). Hosted on Netlify (project "cute-salamander-50d302"), deployed from the `main` branch of this repo.
- **Every push to `main` is a Netlify production deploy and costs 15 credits.** The owner asked that changes be batched.
- Do all work on the `drafts` branch. Push `drafts` freely (it does not publish).
- Only merge `drafts` into `main` and push when the owner explicitly says to publish.
- Preview drafts without a deploy: run `python3 tools/build.py` and show `preview.html` (sample stock).
- Edit `tools/index.template.html`, then run `python3 tools/build.py` to regenerate `public/index.html`. Do not hand-edit `public/index.html`.
- Live stock comes from KORONA via `netlify/functions/stock.mjs`, which serves a saved copy from Blobs ("stock-latest") instantly and refreshes it in the background when over 60s old; `stock-refresh.mjs` also rebuilds it every 10 min, 6 AM–10 PM Central. Section rules live at the top of that file. Check sorting with `/api/stock?report=<REPORT_KEY>`.
- Store Tools at /food-stock (PIN in FOOD_PIN): tabs Food Stock (switches, Netlify Blobs; jump buttons at the top for each food group, maker and Grilling & Outdoor), Specials (This Week's Specials banner, up to 5 lines, specials.mjs), Sales (On Sale section at the top of the site + red On Sale chip; on/off switch, items with photo/name/sale price/regular price/note, "Save $X" computed; sales.mjs, Blobs "sales" + "sale-photo-<id>", photos shrunk in the browser), Seasonal (after Sales; on/off switch hides it completely; one editable name used for the menu-bar chip, Browse drawer and section heading; items with photo/name/price/note; seasonal.mjs, Blobs "seasonal" + "seasonal-photo-<id>"; section sits right after On Sale), Couldn't Find It, Top Searches, Requests, Bourbon Club, with unread badges. No deploy needed for switch changes. Menu data: tools/menu.js. Functions: food-stock.mjs, insights.mjs, shared PIN check in netlify/lib/pin.mjs.
- Grebe's auto-restock: grebes-restock.mjs sets every Grebe's switch to In Stock each Thursday 9 AM Central (list in netlify/lib/restock.json, generated by tools/build.py from menu.js).
- Sections drawer: 3-line menu button left of the logo (and left of the small search bar when scrolled) opens the "Browse" drawer from the left (ends with a Request an Item link to #request). On Sale section sits right after Made Here listing the same chips as the menu bar; tap jumps there. Menu bar highlights the current section (spy()), centered on phones, two balanced rows on computers.
- Hot Ham banner: HOT_HAM in tools/index.template.html (Sunday 9 AM–3 PM). Home-screen icons in public/img + public/manifest.webmanifest.
- Grilling & Outdoor: fixed list OUTDOOR in tools/menu.js; In Stock unless switched off in Store Tools (switch key "Outdoor: <name>"); prices editable in Store Tools (food-stock blob `prices[key]`, overrides menu.js). Pellets have `variants` (flavors), each its own switch "Outdoor: <name> – <flavor>"; all flavors off = Out of Stock. Owner said 2026-11-03: do NOT add ice (KORONA group ICE stays off the site). Soda (Soda & Mixers → Soda) always shows In Stock, even without KORONA stock history (stock.mjs `alwaysIn`).
- THC section (KORONA group THC) after Seltzers, 21+ only, auto-hides from 2026-12-11 (federal hemp THC limit) via `until` in SECTIONS.
- KORONA tags override sections (TAG_SECTIONS / HIDE_TAGS in stock.mjs). Tag "Allocated Bourbon" -> Whiskey & Bourbon, shown in its own box on the right (top on phones).
- Food photos: JPGs in public/img/food (900x675, 4:3, ~80 quality), set `photo:"file.jpg"` in tools/menu.js; build.py inlines them into preview.html (FOOD_PHOTOS). Owner sends phone photos; correct lighting (sun stripes on the wood board) and crop 4:3 around the dish. Rotate so the wood grain runs side to side (owner wants all photos to match). Packaged products (Local Favorites, vacuum packs): keep package upright, crop 4:3 to the label + top of product, and remove plastic glare; Kraemer cheese blocks use the LIGHT touch only, owner preferred it (photowork kr.py LIGHT=True) (owner approved strong cleanup for vacuum packs 2026-11-04: inpaint white streaks on the product, darken streaks on black plastic toward black, never touch the label; leave the clear edge flap). Food: tame glare lightly only.
- Local Favorites makers: Northern Trails & Kraemer (Watertown), Pernat's (Ixonia), Sausage Haus (Oconomowoc), Grebe's (West Allis). Each flavor is its own card/switch; `name` is the unique switch key, `label` is what shows.
- Any KORONA group or product with "DO NOT USE" (or DNU) in its name is always skipped (RETIRED in stock.mjs). Owner retired groups this way, e.g. "DO NOT USE SODA", "DO NOT USE JUICE".
- Owner preferences: Title Case on names, labels and buttons ("Mac & Cheese", "Each", "In Stock"). Never show "Call to Check".

- FOOD_PIN is set in Netlify. Owner confirmed the address as written: "N27 W26 N26980 Prospect Ave, Pewaukee, WI 53072".
- Cloudflare Web Analytics token is in tools/build.py (live site only, not on Store Tools or the preview).
- Store info (ADDRESS, HOURS) is at the top of tools/index.template.html; the Google info (JSON-LD LiquorStore, hours, address) and sharing tags are in its <head> too, so update both if hours change. robots.txt hides /food-stock and /api; sitemap.xml lists the home page. New Arrivals: stock.mjs records each product's first in-stock date in Blobs (`first-stocked`); first run seeds everything as old.
