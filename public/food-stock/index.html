On Sale

Shows as an "On Sale" section at the top of the website, with an On Sale tab in the menu. Turn it off to hide it; your items stay saved for next time.

Seasonal

A section for the season (Christmas, Fourth of July, Packers Season…). Its name shows in the website's menu bar, the 3-line Browse menu and the section heading. Turn it off and it disappears completely; your items stay saved for next time.

Normal Weekly Hours

One-Day Changes
